At Saginaw Valley State University, I teach computer science through web and mobile development, cybersecurity, and hands-on exploration. My research focuses on location privacy in connected vehicles and the Internet of Things.

My work brings together research, student collaboration, and service to the computing community. Explore my publications or visit my university profile for research and professional activities.

What this rough estimate assumes

Annual medical bills are spread evenly across 12 months. Use last year’s MESSA claims as a starting point: billed medical charges minus provider savings, excluding free preventive services. Do not enter just your deductible or personal payments. No visit counts are needed. Medical visit copays are omitted; medical care is modeled as deductible then 0% coinsurance. The regular prescription category defaults to preferred brand ($40) and can be changed under working numbers. Published monthly working amounts: generic $10; preferred brand $40; nonpreferred brand $80; preferred specialty 20% capped at $150; nonpreferred specialty 20% capped at $300. Copays cannot exceed the modeled remaining drug price. Insured Mounjaro is modeled as a separate Tier 2 fill for each checked person, with no preventive exemption or manufacturer assistance. Direct Mounjaro payments are added outside all insurance accumulators and limits. All coverage and requirements are assumed satisfied. Confirm your 2027 benefit before choosing a plan.

ABC prescriptions count toward the family deductible, then copays continue until the combined maximum. PPO prescriptions have immediate copays and separate $2,000-per-person / $4,000-family limits. ABC family calculations use the stated family combined ceiling. Free preventive prescriptions should be excluded from both prescription entries. Use this as a scenario comparison, not a prediction of exact claims.

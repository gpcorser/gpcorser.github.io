How the estimates work

Solid lines use coverage as entered. Dotted lines price every prescription at its market price and give those payments no deductible credit. Both line charts show premiums and claims before FSA adjustments.

Each prescription represents one monthly fill. The covered amount is based on the negotiated price and selected tier. Medical bills are spread across the year and processed before prescriptions. The model excludes medical visit copays and manufacturer assistance. Premiums use the supplied two-person rates. Verify coverage and negotiated prices with MESSA.

The PPO chart assumes the full Healthcare FSA election is available at the start of the year. Eligible market-price prescriptions can be reimbursed even if insurance did not pay. Use the amount you actually pay, after any other reimbursement. Payroll deposits appear once, and reimbursements reduce the remaining expenses. The model includes the full election even when some is unused. It excludes tax savings and carryover. HSA Catchup is modeled only for the preferred ABC plan. It starts at zero and is funded monthly. The chart counts remaining HSA deposits as cash set aside.
